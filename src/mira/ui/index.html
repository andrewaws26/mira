Step-by-step first-of-night alignment. Each step runs against the live mount. If something fails, troubleshooting hints appear below the step.

1 Start INDI server

Launches indiserver and connects to the Celestron mount. Run this once per session before any motion.

2 Orient north

Drives the OTA northward for ~12 seconds via TELESCOPE_MOTION_NS. Gets the scope roughly pointed at Polaris when the firmware horizon guard would otherwise refuse coordinate slews.

3 Center Polaris (manual)

Use the jog panel (or open Live tab and use arrow keys) to fine-center Polaris in the eyepiece. When centered, hit Next to record this pointing as the sync reference.

4 Sync

Captures a frame, plate-solves it with ASTAP, and syncs the mount to the solved coordinates. Replaces the fake alignment with a real one. Needs actual stars in the frame.

5 Confirm

Test by slewing to a bright known target.

Smart capture: pick a target, Mira classifies it and runs the right pipeline.
